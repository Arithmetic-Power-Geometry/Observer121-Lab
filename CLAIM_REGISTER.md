# Observer121 Claim Register

## Primary claim permitted

Observer121 provides a common controlled benchmark for attributing an apparent gain in observation productivity to declared experimental freedoms using matched interventions and explicit confounding checks.

## Secondary claims permitted

- In the implemented calibration families, the benchmark correctly distinguishes effects due to adaptivity, query-language richness, representation, and selection policy when those factors are independently controlled.
- Equivalent procedural compilation eliminates the claim that online generation itself supplies a unique query-count advantage in the tested family.
- In the 11 x 11 additive calibration, 21 cells suffice under the additive promise, while exact certification against an unrestricted single-cell deviation requires all 121 cells.
- Risk-controlled auditing between those extremes follows the standard finite-population hypergeometric assurance calculation.
- A standard real-data classification experiment tests the matched-selection comparison outside analytically constructed identification families.

## Claims not permitted

Do not describe Observer121 as a new universal theory of information, a new active-learning algorithm, a new property-testing theorem, a new optimal-design theorem, or a proof that 121 is universally necessary.

## Writing rule

Use "Observer121" in scientific prose. Version labels are reserved for software releases, archived artifacts, and provenance.
