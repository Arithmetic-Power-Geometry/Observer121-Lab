# V1 succinctness attack

## Survivor under attack

The previous V1 audit showed that online generation and an equivalent pre-written procedural policy have identical observation complexity. The surviving benefit was compact representation relative to explicit materialization.

## Exact test

For n = 2^k equiprobable targets, any exact binary observation policy requires at least k answers. Balanced generated and balanced procedural policies both attain k. We then compare an explicitly materialized full binary tree with the compact procedural description.

## AESHO decision rule

- **Attack:** compare the surviving representation advantage with an exact information lower bound and an equivalent procedural compilation.
- **Eliminate:** reject any claim that online generation provides extra information when the procedural policy matches it.
- **Survive:** retain only the explicit-materialization versus procedural-description gap.
- **Harden:** compare that survivor against established succinct-representation and knowledge-compilation results.
- **Output:** call it a benchmark/engineering benefit unless a domain-specific separation survives the prior-art attack.
